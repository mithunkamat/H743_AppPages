# Delete your H743 account


**H743** by Mithun Kamat

## In the app (fastest)

Open the app → menu (⋮) → **Delete my account…** → **Delete**. It is done at once.

## Without the app

Send an e-mail from the address of your account to mithun.kamat@gmail.com with the subject **"Delete my H743 account"**.
We delete it within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

## What is deleted

- your sign-in (e-mail address, password, two-step sign-in), your account and identity ids;
- your rights on all boards (a board you owned can then be claimed again by someone at the board);
- the record of your active phone.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Board status sent by a board itself is not personal data of yours and stays with the
board's other users.
